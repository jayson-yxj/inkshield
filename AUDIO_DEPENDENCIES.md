# Audio export dependency record

Audio export uses PyAV 16.0.1, installed additively from its official PyPI cp311-win_amd64 wheel (https://pypi.org/project/av/16.0.1/). PyAV source and BSD notice: https://github.com/PyAV-Org/PyAV/tree/v16.0.1 ; the installed LICENSE.txt/AUTHORS are preserved in the bundled third_party_licenses/av directory.

The wheel's dynamically loaded FFmpeg avcodec, avformat and avutil libraries each report `LGPL version 3 or later` via their native *_license() entry points. Configuration includes --disable-static --enable-shared --disable-programs --enable-version3; libavcodec is 62.11.100, libavformat 62.3.100 and libavutil 60.8.100. FFmpeg project/source/license: https://ffmpeg.org/ ; https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg ; https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.html . PyAV binary build recipes and dependencies: https://github.com/PyAV-Org/pyav-ffmpeg . The exact dependency-source/rebuild bundle and all wheel-vendored codec library notices still require a separate redistribution compliance audit before public/commercial distribution. Native license strings are evidence, not a legal clearance. Wheel also ships x264/x265 DLLs; their licensing/source obligations must not be inferred from the LGPL core declaration.

Runtime copies only the processed video stream. Original video is never muxed into exports. The first original audio stream is decoded, resampled to stereo 48 kHz, trimmed at sample boundaries to start_frame/fps and frame_count/fps, padded for timestamp gaps, and encoded to AAC 192 kbit/s. Missing original audio is a successful video-only export; unreadable audio/mux failures fail the export instead of silently dropping audio. Output audio is independent of player mute state.

imageio-ffmpeg 0.6.0 was added only to generate/decode test fixtures. Its bundled FFmpeg 7.1 gyan binary enables GPL/version3; it is explicitly excluded from the shipped runtime. Do not replace PyAV with that executable without resolving its GPL corresponding-source and dependency obligations.
